# Meetings with Pucko

Open **Pucko** from Bara's app dock or account menu. Choose **Start Meeting**,
confirm everyone present consents, and allow microphone access. You can start
directly; Bara manages any agent work for you.

Say **“Citrus”** anywhere in a request: “Citrus, what changed?” or “What do you
think, Citrus?” Pucko follows the discussion and answers out loud when addressed.
After he answers, keep talking: a follow-up or a correction in the next few seconds
needs no name. Say “thanks”, or let the room move on, and he goes quiet again.

You can also address him without the name: tap his face or press Space, then speak.
When it is only you, turn on **Just me** and he answers everything you say.

Ask for research or other work to use your existing Bara agent and its tools. Work
continues while you talk and after the meeting ends. Required approvals appear in
Pucko; detailed conversations remain available through the meeting's work link.

Pucko stays quiet during other conversation. Address him again to ask for a task's
result. If your browser blocks sound, choose **Enable speaker**, then address Pucko again.

In **Pucko settings**, connect an OpenAI project key with GPT-Live access. The key
is saved on the selected Bara server, so configure the Windows server when using
Windows Bara. A coding subscription does not supply voice API access. You can
also change the activation name, project and coordinator there.

Mute stops microphone capture; unmute resumes it. End stops listening and saves
meeting notes with decisions, action items and open questions. Find previous
meetings in **Meeting history** and their saved notes in Library. You can move to
another Bara app during a meeting; the microphone indicator lets you return or end it.

Audio is transmitted to OpenAI while the microphone is on. Bara saves meeting
text and notes on the selected server and does not record raw audio. Closing or
reloading the window stops capture; start a new meeting to listen again.

Pucko is available in the web and desktop apps. A remote browser needs HTTPS for
microphone access. The native mobile app does not yet include Pucko.
